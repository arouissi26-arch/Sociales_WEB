Antonieta's Story

Antonieta is a 62-year-old Italian woman who has always had a very special story. She was born into a family working in the primary sector: her parents were farmers who spent their days tending the land and crops. She grew up surrounded by fields, animals, and the hard work of the countryside.

From a young age, Antonieta discovered she had a great passion for Italian cuisine. She loved watching her mother prepare traditional dishes with fresh ingredients from the farm. She dreamed of cooking for many people one day, but always thought that, due to her social class and background, those dreams were too big and difficult to achieve.

But that didn't stop her. After working hard, she created ''La Cucina di Mamma'', a restaurant with traditional Italian food that reminds her of her mother. Today the restaurant is very popular among tourists and locals. A couple of years ago, Antonieta retired and handed over the reins to four young employees from Spain, with the hope of continuing Antonieta's dream.

Origin of our Food

In our kitchen, we use foods of natural origin, meaning ingredients we harvest directly. We have an agricultural farm in Tuscany. It is an active organic estate where we grow wheat, sunflower seeds, spelt, and chickpeas, among other crops.

In the Maremma plain, we cultivate rice, particularly varieties like Carnaroli or Arborio, taking advantage of the flatter soils and available water for planting.

Agricultural Methods and Philosophy

Wheat

Cultivated by first preparing the land with modern tractors and plows. Sown with uniform seeders.

  • Uniform seeders
  • Automatic irrigation and pest control
  • Harvest with "combine harvesters" (cut, thresh, and separate)

Rice

Requires flooded fields, leveled with special equipment. Varieties: Carnaroli, Arborio.

  • Flooded and leveled fields
  • Harvest by draining water
  • Mechanical grain separation

Sustainable Philosophy

In our Italian restaurant, we believe good cuisine starts in the earth. Small plots ( <1 ha) delineated by natural elements. We prioritize organic farming: no artificial pesticides or GMOs. Organic fertilizers and respect for biodiversity and natural rhythms.

Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Do you accept reservations?

+

Yes! You can book your table through our online system or by calling us directly. We recommend booking in advance, especially on weekends.

🥗 Do you have vegetarian/vegan options?

+

Of course! Our menu includes various vegetarian and vegan options, always prepared with the same quality fresh ingredients. Ask our team for recommendations.

🅿️ Is parking available?

+

We have an agreement with the "Garage Condotti" parking just 50 meters from the restaurant. Our customers get a 30% discount by presenting the restaurant receipt.

💳 What payment methods do you accept?

+

We accept cash, credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), and digital payments like Apple Pay and Google Pay.

👶 Is it a family restaurant?

+

Absolutely! We are a family restaurant and love welcoming families with children. We have high chairs, a special kids' menu, and changing tables in the restrooms.

La Chef Antonieta

Nascuda a Roma el 1940, Antonieta va aprendre les receptes tradicionals de la seva àvia. El 1962, amb només 22 anys, va obrir La Cucina di Mamma amb el somni de compartir l'autèntica cuina italiana amb el món.

🏠

Història del Restaurant

Des de 1962, hem estat un pilar de la gastronomia romana. Tres generacions de la família han continuat la tradició, mantenint les receptes originals mentre abracem ingredients de temporada.

🎯

Filosofia Culinària

"Menjar no és només alimentar-se, és crear records." Creiem en la cuina honesta, amb ingredients locals i de qualitat. Cada plat és una abraçada de la Mamma.

🌾

Origen dels Ingredients

Els nostres ingredients vénen directament de granges locals de la Toscana, Sicília i el Lazio. Pasta artesanal, tomàquets cultivats al sol, oli d'oliva extra verge DOP.

❤️

Per què ho fem

Perquè cada somriure d'un client ens recorda per què vam començar. Perquè la cuina italiana és amor fet realitat. Perquè som, i sempre serem, La Cucina di Mamma.
